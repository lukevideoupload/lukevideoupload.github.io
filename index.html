Luke Video Upload

Luke Video Upload is a publishing tool for short-video creators. It lets a creator upload and post their own original videos to their own TikTok account.

The tool uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API). A creator signs in with their own TikTok account, authorizes the tool, and publishes their own original short videos — for example, original horror-story narrations and data-ranking videos — to their own account.

The tool only ever accesses the authorized creator's own account and content. It does not post to anyone else's account, and it does not collect data from any TikTok user other than the creator who signed in.
