Luke Video Upload

Luke Video Upload is a personal tool created and used solely by its owner, LUKE, an independent video creator.

The tool uses TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API) to upload and publish the owner's own original short videos — original horror-story narrations and data-ranking videos — to the owner's own TikTok accounts (@luke.kaidan and @luke_ai_ranking).

This tool is not a public service. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, any third party. It does not collect data from any TikTok user other than the owner.
